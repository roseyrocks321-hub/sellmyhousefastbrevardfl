How We Calculate Your Cash Offer

Our cash offers are based on four factors specific to your property and the Brevard County market:

1. After Repair Value (ARV)

We research comparable sales of similar homes in your neighborhood that have been fully updated. This tells us what your house would sell for in top condition.

2. Repair and Renovation Costs

We estimate what it will cost us to bring your property to market-ready condition. This includes everything from minor cosmetic updates to major structural repairs.

3. Selling Costs

After we buy and renovate your home, we still have to sell it. We factor in realtor commissions, closing costs, and holding costs during the resale period.

4. Our Minimum Profit

As a business, we need to make a reasonable profit for the risk and work involved. This is typically built into the gap between our offer and the ARV.

📊 The Formula:
Cash Offer = ARV − Repair Costs − Selling Costs − Minimum Profit

Example: $400,000 (ARV) − $45,000 (Repairs) − $28,000 (Selling) − $35,000 (Profit) = $292,000 Cash Offer

What Affects Your Offer in Brevard County?

  • Location: Beachside properties (Cocoa Beach, Satellite Beach) command different values than mainland homes
  • Condition: Homes needing major repairs (roof, foundation, HVAC) receive lower offers
  • Market Trends: Current Brevard County median home price and days on market
  • Situation: Foreclosure and probate sales may have additional timeline pressures
  • Size and Features: Square footage, lot size, pool, garage, age of home

Why Cash Offers Are Lower (And Why That's Okay)

  • You're trading price for certainty (no financing fall-through risk)
  • You're trading price for convenience (no repairs, showings, or cleaning)
  • You're trading price for privacy (no MLS listing or public marketing)
  • You're saving $20,000-$30,000 in commissions, fees, and holding costs

Is a Cash Offer Right for You?

A cash sale makes the most sense if you:

  • Need to sell quickly (foreclosure, relocation, divorce)
  • Have a property that needs significant repairs
  • Inherited a property you don't want to manage
  • Are going through probate and need to settle the estate
  • Have bad tenants and want to sell the rental
  • Simply want the easiest, fastest sale possible
