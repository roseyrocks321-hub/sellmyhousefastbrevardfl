No. Unlike selling with a realtor (who charges 6% in commissions), we charge zero commissions and zero fees. We typically pay all closing costs. The offer we make is what you receive at closing, minus any mortgage payoff or liens.

Absolutely not. We buy houses as-is in any condition. Foundation issues? Roof leaking? HVAC broken? Outdated kitchen? Hoarder house? We'll handle all repairs after closing. Don't spend a penny fixing anything.

What if my house is in foreclosure?

We can help. You can sell your house anytime before the foreclosure auction. We can close in 10 days or less, which is fast enough to stop most auctions.

Brevard County foreclosure auctions are held every Wednesday at 11 AM at the Titusville Courthouse (518 S. Palm Ave, Titusville, FL 32796). The sooner you contact us, the more options we have to help you keep your equity.

Can I sell an inherited house in probate?

Yes. The personal representative (executor) can sell the property during probate with court approval. We work with probate attorneys throughout Brevard County and can close during or after the probate process.

We understand this is a difficult time and handle everything with sensitivity and compassion. We can also work with multiple heirs to ensure a fair and smooth transaction.
