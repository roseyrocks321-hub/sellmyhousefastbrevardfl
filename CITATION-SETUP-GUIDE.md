## Platform-by-Platform Instructions

### 1. Bing Places
**URL:** `https://www.bingplaces.com`
- Sign in with Microsoft account (or create one)
- Click "Add new business"
- Search for your business — if it exists, claim it. If not, create new
- Enter business name, phone, website
- Select "Real Estate" category
- For address: select "I deliver goods and services to my customers" → add Brevard County service areas
- Verify via postcard or phone

### 2. Apple Business Connect
**URL:** `https://businessconnect.apple.com`
- Sign in with Apple ID
- Click "Add a business"
- Search — claim if exists, create if not
- Enter name, phone, website
- Category: Real Estate Services → Real Estate Investor
- Add service area: Brevard County, FL
- Verify via postcard

### 3. Facebook Business Page
**URL:** `https://business.facebook.com`
- Click "Create Account" or use existing
- Go to `facebook.com/pages/create`
- Page name: `Sell My House Fast Brevard FL`
- Category: Real Estate Investor
- Description: (paste from above)
- Add phone, website, hours
- Add profile photo (logo) and cover photo
- Publish the page

### 4. Yelp for Business
**URL:** `https://biz.yelp.com`
- Click "Claim your business"
- Search "Sell My House Fast Brevard FL"
- If not found, click "Add your business to Yelp"
- Enter name, address (use your home/service address or select service-area), phone, website
- Category: Real Estate Services
- Verify via phone call

### 5. Better Business Bureau (BBB)
**URL:** `https://www.bbb.org/get-accredited`
- Click "Get Accredited" or "Apply for Accreditation"
- Enter business name, city, state (FL)
- If already listed, claim it. If not, apply
- BBB charges a fee for accreditation (~$400–600/year), but **listing is free without accreditation**
- If you just want the free listing, search your business on bbb.org first and claim if it exists

## Why This Matters

Every time your exact NAP appears on a trusted directory, Google gains confidence that your business is real and established. Inconsistent NAP (different name, wrong phone, old website) **hurts** your rankings. Consistent NAP across 10+ directories is a baseline requirement for ranking in the local map pack.

**Total time estimate:** 2–3 hours if you do them all in one session.
