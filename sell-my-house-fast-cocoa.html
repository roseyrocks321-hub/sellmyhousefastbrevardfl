About Cocoa, Florida

Founded in 1882, Cocoa is one of Brevard County's oldest communities and serves as the county seat. Located on the western shore of the Indian River, this historic city combines small-town charm with Space Coast excitement. The population of approximately 19,500 residents enjoys a blend of historic architecture, waterfront activities, and proximity to major employers.

Cocoa's historic downtown, known as Cocoa Village, features unique shops, restaurants, and entertainment venues. The city hosts popular events including the Cocoa Village Oktoberfest, Friday Fest concerts, and the historic Cocoa Village Playhouse productions. The nearby Indian River provides opportunities for boating, fishing, and water sports.

The local economy benefits from proximity to Kennedy Space Center, Port Canaveral (one of the world's busiest cruise ports), and Patrick Space Force Base. Major employers include aerospace contractors, healthcare facilities, and educational institutions. This economic diversity supports a stable housing market with consistent investor interest.

For homeowners looking to sell, Cocoa's location advantages, historic appeal, and steady population growth make it attractive to cash buyers and investors.
