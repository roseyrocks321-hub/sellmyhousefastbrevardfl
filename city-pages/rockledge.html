About Rockledge, Florida

Rockledge holds the distinction of being the oldest city in Brevard County, founded in 1876. Named after the coquina rock ledges along the Indian River, this historic community has grown into a desirable residential area while maintaining its connection to the Space Coast's heritage. The city's location along US-1 and proximity to I-95 make it convenient for commuting to Melbourne, Cocoa, and Kennedy Space Center.

The community offers excellent schools, numerous parks including McLarty Park and Taylor Park, and easy access to the Indian River for boating and fishing enthusiasts. Rockledge's mix of historic homes and newer developments creates a diverse housing market that appeals to families, retirees, and professionals alike. The city's established neighborhoods with mature oak trees and landscaping provide a sense of permanence that newer communities often lack.
