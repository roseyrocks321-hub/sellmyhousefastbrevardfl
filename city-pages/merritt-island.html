Merritt Island Neighborhood Deep Dives

We buy houses in every Merritt Island neighborhood. Here's what you should know about selling in each area:

🏖️ South Merritt Island

South Merritt Island encompasses the area south of SR 520 toward the Mathers Bridge. This neighborhood features a mix of established homes from the 1970s-1990s and newer construction. Many properties here are canal-front or have river access. Homes in this area typically range from $320K-$550K, with waterfront properties exceeding $650K. The area is popular with boaters and families seeking proximity to A-rated schools like Edgewood Jr/Sr High and Tropical Elementary.

Selling tip: Canal maintenance and seawall condition significantly impact buyer interest in this area. If your property needs dock repairs, a cash sale may be ideal.

🚀 North Merritt Island

North Merritt Island stretches from SR 528 northward toward the Brevard-Volusia line. This area includes newer developments and is popular with aerospace professionals working at Kennedy Space Center and Blue Origin. Home prices here average $380K-$480K, with many properties built in the 2000s. The northern location offers easy access to Titusville and the beaches.

Selling tip: NASA, SpaceX, and Blue Origin contractor relocations are common here. If you're facing a job transfer, we can close fast enough to match your timeline.

⚓ Newfound Harbor Area

The Newfound Harbor area is one of Merritt Island's most prestigious waterfront communities. Homes here often feature deep-water dockage, pool decks, and direct access to the Banana River. Properties typically sell for $550K-$1.2M+, with some estates exceeding $2M. This neighborhood attracts boating enthusiasts and those seeking luxury waterfront living.

Selling tip: High-end waterfront properties can take months to sell traditionally. A cash offer provides certainty and eliminates ongoing maintenance costs during listing periods.

🏡 Georgiana Area

The Georgiana area offers some of Merritt Island's most affordable housing options, with homes typically ranging from $280K-$395K. This established neighborhood features mature trees, larger lot sizes, and a mix of architectural styles from mid-century to modern. Many properties here have canal access or are a short drive from public boat ramps.

Selling tip: Older homes in Georgiana may need updates to compete on the MLS. Selling as-is to cash buyers eliminates renovation costs and speeds up your sale.

🌊 Sykes Creek Area

Properties along Sykes Creek represent some of Merritt Island's most desirable waterfront real estate. This area includes both direct creek-front homes and canal properties with Sykes Creek access. Prices range from $425K for canal homes to $850K+ for creek-front estates. The area offers quick boat access to the Indian River and Atlantic Ocean.

Selling tip: Waterfront maintenance—including seawalls, docks, and boathouses—can be expensive. If deferred maintenance has accumulated, a cash sale lets you transfer those responsibilities to the buyer.

🌴 Indian River Drive

Indian River Drive is Merritt Island's premier waterfront corridor, featuring stunning direct river views and deep-water dockage. These properties command top dollar, with prices ranging from $650K to over $2M for estate homes. The scenic drive offers panoramic river views and is one of Brevard County's most beautiful roadways.

Selling tip: Riverfront properties have unique selling challenges, including flood insurance requirements and seawall inspections. Cash buyers familiar with waterfront real estate can navigate these complexities quickly.
